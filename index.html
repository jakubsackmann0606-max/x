Our Story

A Proper Local, Since [1998]

Old Williams was built for slow evenings and good company: dark wood, warm brick, a fire that never quite goes out, and a pint poured the way it should be, with patience.

Whether you're here for the match, a round of darts, or a quiet whiskey in the corner booth, there's always a seat and always a welcome. Come in a stranger, leave a regular.
